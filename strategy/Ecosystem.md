# Guidepost Labs • Ecosystem brief

## What the brand promises

**Navigate Crypto Together.** Guidepost Labs is a community-driven Web3 product studio. People bring questions, share opportunities and collaborate. The studio gives recurring problems a product owner and a defined scope. The community has a reason to exist even for someone who never uses an NFT tool.

Use the studio name for ownership and accountability, Guidepost for the shared brand and each product name for a specific job. An appropriate attribution on an independent product is “A Guidepost Labs product.” Keep an accessible route from each future product site back to the directory. A product retains its own release cadence and documentation.

| Visitor's question | Product | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| When does the mint open? | MintPulse, an NFT mint-reminder Discord bot | Confirm scheduling, installation and delivery before promising specific features. |
| Which of my addresses qualify? | DropCheck, a privacy-first analyser for OpenSea-hosted drops | A written engineering specification establishes intended scope. The founder confirms final-stage development and a planned public launch. Verify live data access before publishing an app link. |
| What handles minting? | Strike, an NFT minting bot | The founder confirms completion and restricted access. Confirm chains, execution model, access and owner before describing the workflow. |

The ordering helps a visitor select a tool by task. It establishes no integration, shared wallet, shared login or dependency between products. Avoid partner language around OpenSea. Refer to it as the platform covered by DropCheck.

## Community into product work

Proposed operating practice, subject to founder approval. A moderator collects a short problem report with the attempted task, point of failure and workaround. A studio owner groups similar reports, checks frequency with other members and writes a one-page brief with a success condition. A product owner tests a narrow solution with volunteers. The studio returns a dated update to the original discussion with the outcome and an invitation for corrections.

For example, an address that appears eligible for one phase but has no result for another is a useful DropCheck test case. An owner can test whether a user distinguishes an unknown result from confirmed ineligibility. Use sample addresses in shared examples. Keep member identities and private submissions out of public notes unless contributors approve attribution.

## Information architecture and visitor journeys

| Destination | First question answered | Next meaningful action |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Who is behind this and why visit? | Explore a product or the community. |
| Products | Which tool fits my task? | Open the specific brief. |
| Product brief | What problem does this address and is it accessible? | Read scope and availability. Add a verified access link at release. |
| Community | What can I contribute? | Read the example prompt and join the official Discord. |
| Studio | How do community needs relate to product decisions? | Compare examples in the directory. |

Keep broader Web3 discovery on Home and Community. Use NFT-specific language within the directory. Avoid a site-wide “Connect wallet” action. Add a product update only when there is an owner, a date and a verifiable change. Publish a separate updates index once there are enough entries to browse.

## Visual and editorial direction

Use the supplied lockup on the masthead, the square icon for compact placements and the wide banner for social headers. Preserve proportions, original pixels, transparency and embedded typography. Use the asset dimensions in the files when sizing exports: the supplied banner measures 2048 × 682, the icon 1024 × 1024 and the lockup 956 × 384. Inspect platform crops before posting.

The prototype uses near-black #050C12 for the canvas, #0B1921 for surfaces, #69D0D9 for category labels and #4DD669 for the primary action. These are proposed interface colors, not official logo specifications. One accented action per introductory panel makes product discovery easy to find. Text labels carry meaning independently of color. Mobile layouts keep the full navigation visible and stack product cards in reading order.

Use a concrete verb for each product. MintPulse reminds, DropCheck checks and Strike mints. In pre-release copy, describe the intended job and identify availability separately. Reserve “live,” “private beta,” and performance or privacy claims for evidence reviewed by the product owner.
